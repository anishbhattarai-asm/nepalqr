Does this actually work?

Confirmed for Fonepay at one point in time: an injected amount pre-filled and a payment settled. That is not a guarantee for every bank. Another acquirer may sign its payloads and reject an edited amount. Always test with a tiny amount (e.g. Rs 1) on your own QR before relying on it. The check above flags fields that look like issuer signatures, but only a live scan truly settles it.
